1. PRIORITY SYSTEM OVERVIEW
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every concept, problem, and formula in this guide is prioritized using a 5-tier
matrix based on PYQ frequency (Spring 2024, Autumn 2025, Spring 2025), marks yield,
and teacher lecture emphasis:

- 🔴 PRIORITY A (MUST STUDY):
  Guaranteed exam questions (22–26 marks out of 30).
  * Successive Differentiation & Leibniz Theorem (6 Marks, Q3a)
  * Related Rates (Circle area/radius rate of change, 4 Marks, Q2c)
  * Rolle's Theorem & Lagrange MVT Verification (4 Marks, Q3b)
  * First Principle Derivatives (sin x, e^x, ln x, 3 Marks, Q2a)
  * Piecewise Differentiability Testing (3 Marks, Q1c)
  * Indeterminate Forms & L'Hopital's Rule (3 Marks, Q1d)
  * Domain & Range of absolute values & radicals (2 Marks, Q1b)
  * Maclaurin Series Expansion (log(1+x), cos x, 2 Marks, Q1a)

- 🟠 PRIORITY B (HIGH IMPORTANCE):
  High-probability alternative questions and sectional marks.
  * Derivative of one inverse trig function w.r.t another (tan^-1 w.r.t sin^-1)
  * Infinite nested roots and powers (sqrt(sin x + ...), (cos x)^((cos x)^...))
  * Piecewise continuity testing at points (removable/jump discontinuities)

- 🟡 PRIORITY C (MEDIUM IMPORTANCE):
  Supporting concepts: Taylor's Series, even/odd functions, one-sided limit tests.

- 🟢 PRIORITY D (LOW IMPORTANCE):
  Elementary graph sketching and full mathematical induction proofs.

- ⚪ PRIORITY E (OPTIONAL):
  Supplementary textbook exercises.
