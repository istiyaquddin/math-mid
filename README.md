## 🎯 1. Priority System Overview
Every concept, problem, and formula in this guide is prioritized using a 5-tier matrix based on Previous Year Questions (PYQ) frequency, marks yield, and teacher lecture emphasis:

- 🔴 **PRIORITY A (MUST STUDY)**: Guaranteed exam questions (22–26 marks out of 30).
  - Successive Differentiation & Leibniz Theorem (6 Marks, Q3a)
  - Related Rates (Circle area/radius rate of change, 4 Marks, Q2c)
  - Rolle's Theorem & Lagrange MVT Verification (4 Marks, Q3b)
  - First Principle Derivatives (sin x, e^x, ln x, 3 Marks, Q2a)
  - Piecewise Differentiability Testing (3 Marks, Q1c)
  - Indeterminate Forms & L'Hopital's Rule (3 Marks, Q1d)
  - Domain & Range of absolute values & radicals (2 Marks, Q1b)
  - Maclaurin Series Expansion (log(1+x), cos x, 2 Marks, Q1a)

- 🟠 **PRIORITY B (HIGH IMPORTANCE)**: High-probability alternative questions and sectional marks.
  - Derivative of one inverse trig function w.r.t another
  - Infinite nested roots and powers
  - Piecewise continuity testing at points

- 🟡 **PRIORITY C (MEDIUM IMPORTANCE)**: Supporting concepts: Taylor's Series, even/odd functions, one-sided limit tests.
- 🟢 **PRIORITY D (LOW IMPORTANCE)**: Elementary graph sketching and full mathematical induction proofs.
- ⚪ **PRIORITY E (OPTIONAL)**: Supplementary textbook exercises.

1. Read the **Formula Master Sheet** to review all definitions & memory tricks.

3. Pay close attention to "🟢 সহজ বাংলায় বুঝি" and "⚠️ Common Exam Mistakes".

- **Hour 1:** Master Leibniz derivations ($y = x^n \ln x$ and $y = \tan^{-1} x$). *(6 Marks)*
